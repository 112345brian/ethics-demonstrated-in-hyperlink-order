Editorial and textual notes keyed from the Ethics text. Node dossiers pull directly from these anchors where a node links to a note.

Curley’s term index and explanatory glossary. Node dossiers surface glossary entries linked from each proposition, definition, or axiom.

Reference List

The edition’s bibliography, including Curley, Gueroult, Wolfson, Bennett, Joachim, Matheron, and other major resources cited in the notes and glossary.
