IVP2

[10] P2: We are acted on, insofar as we are a part of Nature, which cannot be conceived through itself, without the others.

Uses

  • IIID2D2: I say that we act when something happens, in us or outside us, of which we are the adequate cause, i.e. (by D1), when something in us or outside us follows from our nature, which can be clearly and [10] distinctly understood through it alone. On the other
  • IIID1D1: I call that cause adequate whose effect can be clearly and distinctly perceived through it. But I call it partial, or inadequate, if its [5] effect cannot be understood through it alone.
