proposition

IIIP23

P23: He who imagines what he hates to be affected with Sadness will rejoice; if, on the other hand, he should imagine it to be affected with Joy, he will be saddened. And both these affects will be the greater or lesser, as its contrary is [30] greater or lesser in what he hates.

Uses

  • IIIP20[II/156] P20: He who imagines that what he hates is destroyed will rejoice.
  • IIIP13[20] P13: When the Mind imagines those things that diminish or restrain the Body’s power of acting, it strives, as far as it can, to recollect things that exclude their existence.
  • IIIP27[5] P27: If we imagine a thing like us, toward which we have had no affect, to be affected with some affect, we are thereby affected with a like affect.
