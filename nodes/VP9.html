proposition

VP9

P9: If an affect is related to more and different causes which the Mind considers [15] together with the affect itself, it is less harmful, we are less acted on by it, and we are affected less toward each cause, than is the case with another, equally great affect, which is related only to one cause, or to fewer causes.
