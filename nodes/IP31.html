IP31

[30] P31: The actual intellect, whether finite or infinite, like will, desire, love, etc., must be referred to Natura naturata, not to Natura naturans.63

Study Dossier

1 notes4 glossary terms0 resources

Curley / Editorial Notes

Note 63

63 I.e., though thought is an attribute of God, and he is a thinking thing (IIP1), he has neither intellect, nor will, desire nor love. This doctrine goes back to the Short Treatise (cf. I/45/21ff.).
