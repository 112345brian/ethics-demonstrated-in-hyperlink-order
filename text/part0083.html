Works Cited

Although the editions listed below are the ones I have used, where possible I have made my references in a way which will be independent of the particular edition used, so that someone who has access to another edition, but not the one I have used, will be able to follow the reference. So, for example, references to Maimonides’ Guide of the Perplexed are to the Part and Chapter of the text, and not to the Volume and Page of the Pines edition.
