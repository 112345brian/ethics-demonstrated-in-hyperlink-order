Note on This EPUB

This digital reading edition preserves the text and citation apparatus while adapting the book's form for reflowable EPUB reading. Print page locations are retained as page-break anchors and are available through the EPUB page list.

The five-line print markers in the source text have been retained as internal anchors but are hidden from normal reading. This keeps existing line-level references addressable without interrupting the reflowed text with recurring bracketed line numbers.

Paragraph-level anchors have been added throughout the EPUB to provide stable digital locations for links, annotations, and future index refinement. These anchors do not alter the wording of the text.

Internal references to propositions, definitions, and axioms in the Ethics have been linked to their targets where unambiguous. References to definitions and axioms use canonical citation anchors rather than merely pointing to nearby page or line markers.
